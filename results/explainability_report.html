Key Findings

Frozen sensors are the strongest signal

Frozen detection (rolling std = 0) is the top feature category in 12 of 35 windows, reaching 99% dominance in P20 and P25 where LIT401 was completely stuck. This single feature type catches attacks that all other features miss.

Physical contradictions catch actuator attacks

The sustained contradiction feature (pump ON + no flow over 30s) is decisive for actuator-control attacks. In P13, MV301 contradiction accounts for 49% of total error — the model learned that valve state and flow must agree.

Stage 5 is the most targeted

Stage 5 (reverse osmosis) appears as the primary affected stage in 15 of 35 windows. Its many interconnected sensors (AIT501-504, FIT501-504, PIT501-503) create a rich signal for both direct manipulation and cascading effects.

3 missed windows share a pattern

P10, P11, and P23 are all missed by both models. They share subtle, distributed anomalies across many sensors without a single strong signal — the hardest type for reconstruction-based detection.
